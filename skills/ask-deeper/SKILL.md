# /ask-deeper

Look back over our discussion: **Is there a deeper question we keep circling without actually asking or analyzing it?**

- Offer the strongest candidate, grounded in what we have repeatedly revisited or taken for granted; treat it as a hypothesis.
- Briefly explain how answering it could change our approach, then suggest the smallest useful way to investigate it.
- If the discussion does not support a deeper question, say so; do not force a reframe.
